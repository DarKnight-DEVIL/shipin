How It Works

Paste the product link from any Indian e-commerce store. Tell us your quantity, preferred shipping method, and your delivery address. Our system ingests the request and starts the procurement pipeline automatically.

We'll share a transparent quote covering product cost, domestic Indian transport, international shipping, and our service fee. Pay securely through our integrated payment system. No surprises, no hidden charges.

Your item arrives at our Indian warehouse. We inspect, pack, and ship it internationally. Track every step in real-time. Estimated delivery: 7 to 14 business days depending on destination.

Have multiple purchases? We combine them into a single shipment, reducing your total shipping cost by up to 40%. Simply select "consolidate" in your dashboard.
